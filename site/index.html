Keep the work
in one window.

MetaTrooper runs the coding agents you already use, side by side. See what each is doing, compare their changes, and answer when they need you.

Your engines. Your logins. Runs and history stored on your machine. No MetaTrooper account.

They work.
You make the calls.

A run stops at a gate when it needs your decision. See the next action, then approve, edit or reject.

A fix is done
when it has proof.

  1. 01

    Review the same change.

    Two engines inspect the branch. See where their findings agree and where they differ.

  2. 02

    Choose what gets fixed.

    The run stops at a gate. Pick the findings to act on, then the agent makes the change.

  3. 03

    Make the fix prove itself.

    A test fails without the fix and passes with it. Review the result before approving the hand-back.
