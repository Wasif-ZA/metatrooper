# cc launch evidence

Research checked 18 November 2026. Numbers below describe other launches, not a forecast for cc. “50+ points” is a proxy for attention, not a measured front-page rate. The public HN data cited here does not record front-page placement.

## Opened sources

1. **Show HN, last-year census.** URL: https://www.thegrowthlist.co/launch/best-time-to-post-on-hacker-news . Author: The Growth List (individual author not stated). Date: November 2026, data through 15 November 2026. Measured: final points for Show HN posts by day, hour and link destination. Sample: 48,923 posts over 365 days; 3,352 in the noon Eastern hour; 17,535 linking to GitHub. Exact numbers used: “2.9% (1,423)” reached 50+; “12 PM ET (4.4% reached 50 points, 3,352 posts)”; “github.com | 17,535 | 3.5% | 1.8%”. **Trust: high.** Full-period count, named window and method; timing is association, not causation.

2. **Show HN historical analysis.** URL: https://danfking.github.io/blog/2026/04/23/show-hn-by-the-numbers/ . Author: Daniel King. Date: 23 April 2026. Measured: Show HN scores and a GitHub-star-history subset. Sample: 188,085 posts, with the top 500 cross-referenced to GitHub star histories. Exact numbers used: “The median is 2, the 90th percentile is 24, and the 99th percentile is 263”; “After 48h, 92% of the star impact is over.” **Trust: high for points, medium for stars.** Large, repeatable scrape, but the star subset is selected for high scores and capped by the API.

3. **HN effects on AI tool repos.** URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.04453 . Author: Obada Kraishan. Date: 6 November 2025. Measured: GitHub star growth after HN exposure. Sample: 138 repository launches from 2024–2025. Exact numbers used: “an average of 121 stars within 24 hours, 189 stars within 48 hours, and 289 stars within a week”. **Trust: high for the sampled average.** Reproducible public-API analysis with n stated; means are pulled upward by viral cases and do not establish causation.

4. **HN and AI open-source projects.** URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.12643 . Authors: Prachnachai Meakpaiboonwattana, Warittha Tarntong, Thai Mekratanavorakul, Chaiyong Ragkhitwetsagul, Pattaraporn Sangaroonsilp, Raula Kula, Morakot Choetkiertikul, Kenichi Matsumoto and Thanwadee Sunetnanta. Date: 14 June 2025. Measured: HN stories, comments and activity in linked GitHub repos. Sample: 2,195 HN stories, 4,476 comments and 1,814 repositories. Exact numbers used: “2,195 Hacker News (HN) stories”; “1,814 GitHub repositories”. **Trust: high.** Research dataset with a stated sample and published method; it does not give a cc-specific conversion rate.

5. **Impulse.dev launch postmortem.** URL: https://dev.to/kirillrogovoy/how-i-launched-impulsedev-4di3 . Author: Kirill Rogovoy. Date: 12 October 2022. Measured: source-attributed landing-page visits, stars and a later newsletter mention. Sample: one open-source launch, eight Reddit posts. Exact numbers used: “893 unique LP views” from Reddit; “16 unique LP views” from HN; “94 -> 180 Github stars”; “2646 page views” from tldr.tech. **Trust: medium.** First-person postmortem with specific counts; channels ran together and some referrers were unknown.

6. **Wasp beta launch review.** URL: https://wasp.sh/blog/2023/01/31/wasp-beta-launch-review . Author: Matija Sosic. Date: 31 January 2023. Measured: stars, new projects, local users and channel effects across a launch week. Sample: one product launch. Exact numbers used: “190 GitHub stars added to the repo”; “108 new projects started”; “83 new users”. **Trust: medium.** First-person numbers, but Product Hunt, HN and Reddit were all active in the same week.

7. **Papercups open-source launch.** URL: https://kam-81875.medium.com/launching-an-open-source-project-c5e1fcc14eed . Author: Kam Leung. Date: 25 September 2020. Measured: GitHub stars, hosted signups and repo visitors around its HN launch. Sample: one launch. Exact numbers used: “510” stars before HN; “1500” one week after; “around 5,000 unique visitors that week on our github repo”. **Trust: medium.** First-person before/after counts; unusually successful front-page case, so not a base rate.

8. **Vue.js first week.** URL: https://blog.evanyou.me/2014/02/11/first-week-of-launching-an-oss-project/ . Author: Evan You. Date: 11 February 2014. Measured: daily site visits, repo visits and stars after posts on HN, r/javascript and EchoJS, plus newsletter pickup. Sample: one launch over seven days. Exact numbers used: “Vuejs.org unique visits: 10245”; “GitHub Stars: 212 (+181)”; “GitHub unique visits: 1880”; “Reddit accounts for 10.08%”; newsletter referrals “10.42%”. **Trust: medium.** Detailed first-person daily ledger, but channels overlapped and the result is an exceptional historic case.

9. **Plausible open-source growth account.** URL: https://d3b1x2yrzmar10.devex.com/blog/open-source-saas . Author: Marko Saric. Date: article date not displayed in opened page; account covers 2018–2022. Measured: site visits from HN posts and Product Hunt. Sample: one product with several launch moments. Exact numbers used: “More than 25,000 people visited our site” on an HN day; Product Hunt brought “more than 1,000 visitors and 15 trial signups” on launch day, then “fewer than 20 visitors” a few days later. **Trust: medium.** First-person event metrics, but a mature site and its own web analytics are not directly comparable to a new GitHub repo.

10. **Tweets about open-source repos.** URL: https://cmustrudel.github.io/papers/fang2022twitter.pdf . Authors: Hongbo Fang, Hemank Lamba, James Herbsleb and Bogdan Vasilescu. Date: 2022, ICSE. Measured: association and estimated effects of tweets linking to GitHub repositories on stars and contributors. Sample: “44,544 tweets containing links to 2,370 open-source GitHub repositories”. Exact numbers used: that sample count. **Trust: high.** Peer-reviewed empirical study with controls; its historical Twitter data does not prove a solo maintainer's X post will reach people in 2026.

## Operational reference

GitHub Docs, https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/viewing-activity-and-data-for-your-repository/viewing-traffic-to-a-repository?apiVersion=2022-11-28 and https://docs.github.com/en/rest/metrics/traffic?apiVersion=2022-11-28 , GitHub Inc., current documentation, opened 18 November 2026. They state that repo traffic shows the past 14 days and that views and clones API endpoints return a 14-day daily breakdown. This is product documentation, not launch-outcome evidence.

## Limits by channel

HN has the strongest denominator. The available public score data cannot give an exact front-page rate. Reddit results here are individual, overlapping launch accounts; there is no measured base rate for r/programming, r/LocalLLaMA or r/ClaudeAI. Product Hunt evidence is two single-product accounts. Newsletter evidence is one Impulse pickup and one Vue referral share. The X paper supports a possible effect across many projects, but not a 2026 expected reach. Lobsters, dev.to and GitHub Trending have no reliable incremental launch estimate in the sources opened. Treat them as experiments, not promised growth.
