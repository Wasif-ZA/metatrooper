  • Linear: three assets, not one: wordmark (strongest recognition), logomark for tight spots, icon chip for social. Mostly monochrome, colour kept for backgrounds. Trust: high (their brand page).
  • Zed: three colours only, never recoloured. Trust: high.
  • One bold idea: if one sentence explains the product and also drives the logo, copy and UI, you have a brand. Avoid dark + violet/green gradient + terminal window, the look everyone has. Trust: medium-low (agency author, AI-assisted).
  • Ghostty: a single character (the ghost) carries the whole brand across platforms. Trust: medium (commit history mirror).
